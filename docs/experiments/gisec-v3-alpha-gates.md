# GISEC v3-alpha Relative Gates

The `v3-alpha` gates are relative promotion rules, not vanity thresholds.

## Relative Gates

- `Gate A`
  - `UQ-s` must beat `v1.5 legacy` on the shared evaluation surface.
- `Gate B`
  - `UQ-m` must beat `UQ-s` under the same structure.
- `Gate C`
  - `UR` may only open after the `UQ` base is stable and interpretable.
- `Gate D`
  - `UG` may only open after the `UQ` base is stable and interpretable.
- `Gate E`
  - `UA` may only open after both `UR` and `UG` show positive value separately.

## Why These Gates Exist

- They are relative.
- They are mechanism-focused.
- They are not vanity thresholds.

The project still has an ambitious end target, but stage promotion happens because one stage clearly improves on the last, not because it happens to cross a pretty number early.

## 2026-08-20 23:31 ugnn-e9-train OOM-kill (48G cgroup cap)
Killed at ep1 step 50 after ep0 completed (best.pth saved, val mIoU 0.9635). Root cause: LiteCOCO json.loads holds ~24G of parsed annotations (9.9G train json) in the main process; 16 train + 8 val persistent workers each add ~1.5G anon runtime + COW page divergence (refcount writes on the shared annotation dicts) + 4-deep prefetch queues (~10G in flight). Measured in ugnn-e9-diag: cgroup 39->47G over 400 steps, +2.5G/min, val workers fork only at the first val pass (end of ep0) -> crossed 48G at ep1 step 50. Fix: MemoryMax 96G then 160G (anon 47.5G + reclaimable 53.8G cache measured via memory.stat); workers NOT cut (16 keeps s/step at ~0.40 off the IO bottleneck). Resumed from runs/best.pth at ep1 via --resume-checkpoint/--start-epoch (ported from E8 train_scale.py); smoke ep1 step0 loss 0.161 continuous with ep0 tail. Unit ugnn-e9-train2.
